A 120 year old Oak tree deliberates on questions about solidarity, activism and how to build movements as it witnesses an ongoing one-month protest camp against attacks on refugees and asylum seekers and in memory of the landmark self-organised refugee movement of 2012 at Oranienplatz in Berlin.
