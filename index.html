Pooja Balasubramanian is an independent researcher, writer and filmmaker working at the intersection of feminist political economy, debt and financialisation, and social policy. She holds a PhD in Development Economics from the University of Göttingen and spent five years as a Senior Researcher at the German Institute of Development and Sustainability (IDOS). Her work takes several forms: academic and public Writing, documentary Film, and university Education on the financialisation of development — each returning to a small set of interlinked questions: who debt is owed to, who is made to carry it, and what forms of collective self-determination might exist outside its terms.

Alongside that, Pooja is a freelance theatre artist collaborating with feminist theatre collectives across Germany and India, and has co-curated Exhibition work bringing artists from marginalised communities into conversation with the same themes. That dual practice — rigorous research on one hand, creative and community-facing work on the other — shapes how she approaches every project: as an attempt to make heterodox economic ideas legible and useful to the people they concern, not just to other economists.
