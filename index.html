I’m Dr. Pooja Balasubramanian, an independent consultant and researcher with 12+ years of experience translating rigorous evidence into policy guidance on the care economy, gender-responsive public policy, and social protection, with growing expertise in development and sustainable finance.

I work across:

  • Systematic reviews and meta-analysis for evidence-based policymaking
  • Quantitative and distributional analysis
  • Global value chain analysis
  • Governance and institutional design for gender-sensitive public policy

My work has informed policy and research for UN agencies (UNDP, the Oslo Governance Centre, UNU-WIDER), bilateral development agencies (BMZ), and research institutions (the Center for Global Development, the German Institute of Development and Sustainability), with findings published in journals including World Development and Economic & Political Weekly.

I’m currently expanding into monitoring & evaluation and geospatial data visualisation to support policy diagnostics — happy to discuss project fit.
